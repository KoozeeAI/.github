# KOOZEE

**AI fashion images and videos for e-commerce—from one clothing image to a complete creative workflow.**

KOOZEE helps apparel sellers turn a single clothing image into product photos, AI fashion model images, e-commerce listing creatives, fashion campaign visuals, and marketing videos in one workflow.

Alongside its creation tools, KOOZEE maintains open-source AI Fashion Prompt Libraries: curated, practical starting points for major AI image and video models, built to help fashion teams move from prompt discovery to production.

## Built for Fashion E-commerce

- **Apparel product photos** — create clean, consistent product imagery from a clothing image.
- **AI fashion model images** — place garments on AI models across styles, poses, and settings.
- **E-commerce listing creatives** — produce storefront-ready visuals for product pages and marketplaces.
- **Fashion campaign visuals** — explore editorial directions, seasonal concepts, and branded scenes.
- **Product showcase videos** — turn static apparel assets into motion-led product stories.
- **Social and advertising content** — adapt fashion assets for organic posts, paid media, and short-form campaigns.

## From Prompt to Production

**Discover** a fashion use case → **Copy Prompt** from the library → **Use Prompt** with GPT Image, Nano Banana, Seedance, Kling, or another compatible model → **Generate** your image or video creative.
